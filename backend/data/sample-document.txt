DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM — DataFlow Inc.

Section 1. Purpose. This Addendum governs the processing of personal data
by Processor on behalf of Controller in connection with the Services.

Section 2. Data Minimization. Processor shall process personal data only
to the extent strictly necessary for the purposes set out in Schedule A.

Section 3. Security Measures. Processor shall implement appropriate
technical and organizational measures, including encryption of personal
data at rest and in transit.

Section 4. Sub-processing. Processor may engage sub-processors provided
that Controller is given prior written notice of any intended changes.

Section 5. Breach Notification. Processor shall notify Controller of a
personal data breach without undue delay.

Section 6. Data Subject Rights. [Reserved — process to be finalized in
a future amendment.]

Section 7. International Transfers. Any transfer of personal data outside
the Controller's jurisdiction shall rely on an approved transfer mechanism.
